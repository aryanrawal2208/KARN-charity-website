What we stand for

Karn is a community-driven initiative that collects food, clothes, and funds from generous donors and delivers them directly to families and shelters that need them most. Every rupee and every item is tracked, and every donor gets to see the difference they made.

About Us

KARN started as a small neighbourhood effort and has grown into a network of volunteers who believe that no one should go without food, clothing, or basic dignity. We work with local shelters and schools to make sure every donation reaches someone who truly needs it.
